Reference convention corrected

The initial harness reflected FEMM angles but retained its positive counterclockwise speed. For Coilem's positive clockwise speed, FEMM speed and Back-EMF must reverse: e_public(theta) = -e_FEMM(-theta). This fixed conversion is derived from the pinned flux-linkage derivative code; no sign, phase or scale is fitted.

Convention coilem.femm_clockwise_reference/v2. Raw measurements and the original report are preserved. Root cause and verification.
